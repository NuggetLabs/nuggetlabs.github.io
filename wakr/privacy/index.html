WAKR Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

WAKR is an alarm clock app for Android, made by NuggetLabs. This policy explains what information WAKR uses, where it's kept, and what control you have over it.

In short: WAKR doesn't collect your personal information. What the app needs stays on your phone, and the camera check happens entirely on your phone. Nothing is recorded, and no image is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

What WAKR keeps on your phone

To work, WAKR stores the following on your phone. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see it.

  • Your alarms: their times, days, labels and sounds, whether they're on, and the wake-up task and length you chose.
  • Your wake-up history: when each alarm rang and when it stopped; whether you finished the task, used the emergency exit or missed the alarm; and whether it was too dark for the camera. WAKR works out your streak from this.
  • Your setup: your answers to the setup questions, the plan you chose, and which setup steps you've completed.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark appearance.

The camera

WAKR uses your phone's camera to check that you're up and moving before your alarm stops, and during the practice run. The camera image is analyzed on your phone as it happens. No photos or video are recorded or saved, and no image leaves your phone. The camera is only on while the wake-up task or the practice run is on screen.

Permissions

WAKR asks for these permissions, for these reasons only:

  • Camera: for the wake-up task and the practice run, as described above.
  • Notifications: Android shows your alarm, including the alarm screen, through a notification.
  • Alarms and reminders: so your alarm rings at exactly the time you set.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the alarm screen over your lock screen.
  • Running in the foreground: to keep your alarm ringing until you turn it off.
  • Running at startup: to set your alarms again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keeping the phone awake: while your alarm rings.

WAKR may also ask you to change some of your phone's settings, such as battery optimization and Do Not Disturb, so your alarm isn't delayed or silenced. These are settings on your phone, and changing them doesn't give WAKR any information.

WAKR also has Android's standard permission to use the internet. The current version doesn't send any of your information over it. WAKR doesn't use your location, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, store, sell or share personal information. WAKR has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account to use it.

Google Play

You get WAKR from Google Play, and Google Play handles any purchase you make in the app. Google's use of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details.

Google also gives app developers anonymous, combined statistics, such as how many people installed an app, and crash reports from people who chose to share diagnostic data with Google. We use these only to find and fix problems.

Backups

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include WAKR's data in your phone's backup to your Google Account. Google stores that backup, not us, and you can manage it in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting your information

WAKR's information stays on your phone until you delete it. To delete all of it, uninstall WAKR, or clear its data in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → WAKR → Storage). We have no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Security

Because WAKR's information stays on your phone, it's protected by Android, which keeps each app's data private from other apps, and by your phone's lock screen and encryption.

Children

WAKR isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If WAKR changes in a way that affects your information, for example by adding an optional backup of your streak, we'll update this policy before the change reaches the app, and change the date at the top.

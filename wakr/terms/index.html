What WAKR does

WAKR is an alarm clock. When an alarm rings, it keeps ringing until you complete a movement task in front of your phone's camera, or use the emergency exit. There's no snooze. Completing the task builds your streak. Using the emergency exit, or missing an alarm, ends it.

The emergency exit

The emergency exit is always available while an alarm is ringing. Holding it for 3 seconds stops the alarm, and ends your streak. Use it whenever you can't safely do the movement, for example if you're ill or injured, or something's wrong.

The movement check

The movement check is automated and runs on your phone. It can't always tell that you're moving: for example in poor light, when your phone can't see enough of you, or when the camera isn't available. WAKR tells you what it needs, such as more light or stepping back, and the emergency exit is always there.
